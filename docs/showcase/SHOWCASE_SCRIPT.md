# Opporbyte — showcase before entering the product

Approved script for the 35-second introduction. Implemented as separately
composed landscape and portrait films with the authorized Sarah narration,
original music and effects. Editable source is in `videos/opporbyte-showcase`.

## The idea

This showcase tells job seekers that Opporbyte is designed to bring discovery,
resume preparation and application tracking into one career workspace. Start
with the frustration of switching tools, reveal the relief of one place by the
second beat, show the three parts of that workflow, then invite the visitor in.

Core message: **Your whole job hunt, in one place.**

Keep the established cream, orange, ink, cyan and warm-white palette, Space
Grotesk/Inter and the sculptural orange O. The scattered-to-workspace story is
the explanation of the product, not background decoration. Use existing
fictional opportunity, resume, match and status objects as the visual source.

## How it should behave on the website

- Keep **Enter Opporbyte** available from the beginning. The showcase is an
  invitation; visitors can enter whenever they are ready.
- Let the ending hold until the visitor activates the action. Do not open the
  product automatically or loop the whole introduction endlessly.
- Make the words and visuals work with sound off; narration is optional and
  starts only when the visitor enables sound.
- Offer **Replay film** after it finishes. Returning visitors should have
  direct access without watching the sequence again.
- Use a separately composed portrait film on tall viewports. Under reduced
  motion, start paused and keep the poster, captions and entry available.

## Product truth

The script describes the intended platform. The current build is a frontend
prototype with fictional examples. Keep a clear **Product preview** label on
simulated feature scenes and route entry into the existing demo until the actual
product workflow is available. Avoid claiming complete job-source coverage,
guaranteed hiring, live AI matching or automatic applications.

The promise is centralizing the visitor's workflow. It does not assert that
every employer and application channel is already integrated.
