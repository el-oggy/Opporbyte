# Opporbyte showcase implementation

The user's correction replaces the timed slide-style hero with an actual
continuous 35-second product film. Preserve the approved seven-beat script and
the existing cream, orange, ink and cyan brand direction.

## Implementation

Write deterministic Three.js/Canvas motion in `videos/opporbyte-showcase`.
Use the existing sculptural O, physical cards, resume layers and tracking
objects. Bring real captures of the local discovery demo into a dimensional
browser frame. Continuous camera travel and object movement connect the beats.
Render 1920x1080 and a separately composed 1080x1920 at 30 fps.

Generate the approved seven narration clips using the authorized Fish Audio
Sarah voice. Keep narration, original score and effects as editable stems,
mix locally and embed the soundtrack directly in both encoded MP4s. The old
sound failure was caused by references to missing narration files; the native
video now owns one synchronized audio/video stream.

The first viewport is the film. Put sound, pause/replay, captions and product
entry at the top. Remove the bottom chapter strip. Keep entry available from
the beginning, hold the ending, and offer replay. Autoplay begins muted; the
first sound click restarts the film so the opening line is heard. Choose the
format by aspect ratio and contain it without cropping. Preserve time, pause,
sound and completion when switching formats. Pause offscreen or when hidden.
Reduced motion begins paused. A failed media load keeps entry usable.

- Check 320px, 390px, tablet, desktop and short landscape layouts, reduced motion,
