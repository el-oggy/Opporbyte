# Opporbyte product film

This film tells job seekers that Opporbyte brings their search, resume preparation
and application progress into one workspace. The user's correction requires an
actual continuous video, replacing the website's slide-like timed captions.

## Locked direction

35 seconds, landscape 1920x1080 and a separately composed portrait 1080x1920,
30 fps. Full-screen opening film with overlaid controls at the top; direct entry
to the existing frontend demo is always available. No chapter strip or slide UI.
Sound is embedded in the video, initially muted for browser autoplay; enabling
sound is one explicit click. The ending holds and offers replay.

Cream #F4E7D2, orange #FF6B2B, ink #202420, cyan #B9F0EC, white #FFFDF8.
Space Grotesk and Inter from the existing local font packages. Keep the physical
orange O, beveled product objects, continuous camera travel and connected paths.

## Story and source

| Time | Movement | Source and message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-6 | Camera travels among fragmented job, resume and tracker objects | Approved script: too many tools for one job hunt |
| 6-11 | Orange O turns into the connecting hub; curves gather the fragments | Existing brand sculpture: one home for the search |
| 11-16 | Real discovery interface appears inside a dimensional browser frame; focus moves toward a role | Captured local frontend demo, fictional examples |
| 16-21 | Resume object travels alongside the selected role and opens its layers | Existing resume preparation preview; illustrative only |
| 21-26 | Application objects move through Saved, Preparing and Interview columns | Existing tracking preview, no submission claim |
| 26-31 | Camera pulls back to one assembled workspace | Central product promise: less switching, more progress |
| 31-35 | Camera returns to the O and resolves into the invitation | Welcome to Opporbyte; entry remains a real DOM link |

No claims of live AI, complete job-source coverage, guaranteed hiring or automatic
applications. Product preview remains labelled. Preserve the backend, root README
and local-only feature branch.
